Software & Compliance

Fully MCS-Compliant Calculations

At Hydronic Design Ltd, we build all room-by-room heat loss metrics and hydraulic layouts using H2X engineering software. The software directly supports the MIS 3005-D Design Standard, utilizing BS EN 12831-1:2017-1 and local weather station data to auto-populate exact external design temperatures. We provide full technical exports—including Heat Load Reports and Heat Emitter Summaries—enabling a smooth sign-off for your certified installations.

We provide a trusted white label solution for outsourced design. This service is offered to contractors, merchants, architects and consultants.

Compliance & Standards

MCS Compliance Notice

All heat loss calculations and underfloor heating layouts provided by Hydronic Design Ltd are executed within H2X software, configured to strictly adhere to CIBSE and MCS technical frameworks. While our data exports are structured to satisfy MCS sign-off criteria, the ultimate compliance and sign-off on the MCS Installation Database (MID) remain the responsibility of the MCS Certified Lead Contractor overseeing the physical installation.

Our Process

How Our UFH Design Process Works

From your plans to a finished design pack, most trade UFH design jobs move through the same four steps.

1
Send us your plans Upload architectural drawings, a floor plan, or an as-built survey (PDF, DWG, or CAD). Let us know the screed type, floor finishes, and heat source if you have them.
2
We calculate the heat loss — or it's provided If you don't already have a heat loss calculation, we produce one room-by-room in H2X software to BS EN 12831-1:2017-1, using local design temperatures and your actual insulation and glazing specs. Already got one? Send it over and we'll design the layout from that instead.
3
We design the pipe layout Manifold circuits, pipe centres, and total pipe run are laid out for even floor coverage and balanced flow across every zone.
4
You receive the design pack in 48 hours (size permitting) Your layout drawing, manifold schedule, and heat loss documentation — branded with your logo, or left unbranded for white-label.

Trade FAQs

UFH Design FAQs for Trade Professionals

What software do you use for UFH design?

All layouts and heat loss calculations are produced in H2X engineering software, built around the MIS 3005-D design standard and BS EN 12831-1:2017-1.

Will the drawings carry your branding or mine?

White-label as standard — layouts and reports go out unbranded, or with your logo if you'd rather we added it. Your customer never sees our name.

How fast is turnaround?

48 hours is standard from receiving your plans, size permitting. Let us know if a job is time-sensitive and we'll flag whether we can move faster.

Do you handle MCS sign-off?

We provide MCS-ready documentation — Heat Load Reports and Heat Emitter Summaries structured to satisfy MCS criteria — but sign-off on the MCS Installation Database remains the responsibility of the MCS Certified Lead Contractor overseeing the installation.

Can you work from an architect's drawings, or do you need a site survey?

Either. Architectural plans are enough for most jobs; if you've got an as-built survey instead, that works too.

What if the layout needs to change after the job's started?

Send through the update and we'll re-issue the drawing.
